# CE 402 — AI Audit (Group 4)

AI tool audited: Claude (Anthropic), used for the calculation engine, the case-study scripts and report text. Six errors or weaknesses were found in its output. Each row gives the AI output, how it was detected and the correction.

| Error / weakness | AI output | How it was detected | Correction and effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Groundwater in the SPT correction | geotech.spt_corrections() was fed the **design** (August high) water table. For BH-1 and BH-2 this gave founding N″ of 17.6 and 19.3. | Re-read P1: the brief keeps a *measured* and a *design* table. The SPT correction describes the ground when the test was done (October), so σ′<sub>v</sub> and the saturation test must use the measured level. Hand recalculation of σ′<sub>v</sub> at 3.0 m in BH-1 (26.1 + 1.1×18 + 0.4×(20 − 9.81) = 50.0 kPa) matched only the measured level. | Measured table for SPT only; design table kept for bearing and settlement. N″ becomes 18.0 (BH-1) and 20.2 (BH-2); footing widths change by ≤ 0.10 m. |
| 2. Undrained strength not derived from the UU data | Design values c<sub>u</sub> = 30, 35 and 25 kPa for BH-1, BH-2, BH-3 were stated without a derivation. | Benchmark against the test sheet: c<sub>u</sub> = deviator/2 gives 28.5 kPa (UDS-1, 7.2 m) and 36.1 kPa (UDS-2, 9.7 m), i.e. 3.04 kPa/m. Extrapolating to the clay tops (5.8, 7.6, 4.4 m) gives about 24, 29.5 and 20 kPa, all below the AI values. | c<sub>u</sub> = 24 / 29.5 / 20 kPa. The raft weak-layer factor of safety at D<sub>f</sub> = 3.0 m fell from 5.07 to 4.06. |
| 3. Overburden factor of the SPT correction | C<sub>N</sub> = 0.77 log<sub>10</sub>(2000/σ′<sub>v</sub>), not capped. | Compared with the Peck–Hanson–Thornburn form quoted in Das (1920 kPa) and noted that C<sub>N</sub> must be capped at low stress (2.0 is the usual cap). Checked by hand at σ′<sub>v</sub> = 26.1 kPa: the original constant gives 1.45; 1920 gives 1.44. | C<sub>N</sub> = min(0.77 log<sub>10</sub>(1920/σ′<sub>v</sub>), 2.0). The platform states that this is a closed form of the chart, not the chart itself. |
| 4. Angular-distortion limits (IS 1904) | Limits quoted as 1/1300 and 1/1666 for the RC frame. | Compared with the damage criteria of Skempton–MacDonald and Bjerrum, where cracking of infill begins near 1/300 and a stricter value applies to RC frames. 1/1300 does not match either set of values. | 1/300 (steel) and 1/666 (RC frame), entered as user inputs and marked ‘confirm against the standard’. The isolated-footing verdict (1/88) fails under any of these values. |
| 5. Preconsolidation pressure σ′<sub>p</sub> | A numerical maximum-curvature routine returned σ′<sub>p</sub> ≈ 109 kPa. | Plotted e–log σ′ from the oedometer sheet (kg/cm² × 98.07): the curvature maximum fell on the first data point, an edge artefact, not a real break. | σ′<sub>p</sub> ≈ 98 kPa from the intersection of the recompression and virgin lines; OCR = 1.24 at the design water table (0.98 at the May low). Both OC and NC settlements are reported. |
| 6. Net versus gross pressure; report drift | C4 pressure was compared with q<sub>ns</sub> on the gross area (135 kPa). The report also said ‘raft FoS 2.0’, ‘29 benchmark tests’ and ‘clay 4–9 m below base’. | Eccentric load: the check must use the effective area B′ L′ (A′ = 3.05 × 3.44 = 10.5 m², P/A′ = 143.6 kPa) and compare it with net capacity. Report text compared line by line with the script output: min FoS 2.22, 30 + 4 checks, clay 3–9 m. | Text corrected to 143.6 kPa (q<sub>ns</sub> = 145 kPa), FoS 2.22, 30 checks, 3–9 m. |

**Weaknesses that remain.** The IS 6403 shape, depth, inclination and W′ expressions, the IS 2131 C<sub>N</sub> chart and the IS 1904 limits were reproduced from secondary sources and must be read against the standards; the Schmertmann settlement has no independent worked-example benchmark; BH-2 and BH-3 clay parameters are transferred from BH-1; the C4 load and moment are assumed. Checks that did pass: BH-1 interior footing by hand (q = 22.195 kPa; q<sub>ns</sub> general 268.6, local 85.8 kPa), one consolidation sublayer (0.961 mm), 30 engine benchmarks and the app smoke test.
